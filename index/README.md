The Vietnam projects are 37–62: the war room and its standard, the responses, the five presidents with every adviser, the Joint Chiefs at the opening and the fall, the nations and the death toll, Cuba by the day, the demographics, communism after Saigon, the air war and its ten-page workbook, the legacy, Constellation, and the Australian shipyard.

Regenerated from `session-index.mjs` in the Venus repository, not here; edits to these HTML files will be overwritten by the next copy. Change the data there — `templates-war/`, `templates-president/`, `templates-jcs/`, `templates-demographics/` — and copy again.
