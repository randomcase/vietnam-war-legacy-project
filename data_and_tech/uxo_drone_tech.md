# UXO clearance: the technology and the people

- **Failure rate**: about 30% of cluster submunitions ("bombies") did not explode. Laos: about 80 million left; Quang Tri, Vietnam: most of the province contaminated.
- **By hand**: metal detector, foot patrol, a kneeling technician. Still the last step in every method below.
- **Drones with LiDAR**: map the ground under the canopy; craters and trench lines show in the 3D model.
- **Thermal imaging**: at dawn and dusk buried metal heats and cools faster than soil and shows to an infrared camera.
- **Magnetometers**: airborne or towed, find the iron; **ground-penetrating radar** gives depth and shape before digging.
- **The data**: the USAF THOR database (Theater History of Operations), sortie by sortie with coordinates and ordnance, cross-referenced against drone maps to rank the ground by probability.
- **The people**: MAG, HALO Trust, PeaceTrees Vietnam, UXO Lao, NPA. Technicians are local and a large share are women. Farmers photograph what the plough turns up and send coordinates by phone; children learn the bombies by song and puppet show (mine risk education); cleared land goes to coffee, pepper, schools.
- **Casualties since 1975**: about 20,000 in Laos, about 100,000 in Vietnam. **Clearance at the funded pace**: about 1% of contaminated land a year in Laos — a century.
