FLIGHT DECK JERSEYS (US Navy carriers, Vietnam era and since)

YELLOW   aircraft handling officers, plane directors, catapult and arresting-gear officers; the ones who signal
BLUE     plane handlers, chock and chain runners, elevator operators, tractor drivers
GREEN    catapult and arresting-gear crews, air-wing maintenance, hook runners, photographers
RED      ordnance (bombs, rockets, guns) and crash and salvage
PURPLE   aviation fuels ("grapes")
BROWN    plane captains, the air wing's own; one per aircraft
WHITE    safety, landing signal officers, medical, quality assurance, air transfer
BLACK/WHITE checkered  final checkers (in the 1960s these were mostly white)

The Ouija board in flight deck control: a scale deck with cut-out silhouettes; a nut on a plane means it is down for maintenance,
a bolt means it needs fuel, a purple pin means fuelling, a thumbtack on the nose means it is chained.
