# The anti-submarine screen around a carrier

The threat off Vietnam was small — the North had no submarines; Soviet and Chinese boats watched but did not attack — but the screen was kept because the carrier is fragile against one torpedo.

| Ring | Distance | Assets | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outer | 50–100+ nm | P-3 Orion patrol aircraft; nuclear attack submarines | sonobuoy fields ahead of the track; SSNs listening deep, ahead |
| Middle | 15–30 nm | destroyers and escorts | active bow sonar; towed arrays behind the ship, away from its own noise |
| Inner | 1–5 nm | SH-3 Sea King helicopters; S-2 Trackers | dipping sonar to fix depth; Mk 46 torpedoes; depth charges |

Plus the picket: a destroyer or cruiser on radar picket north of the carrier (PIRAZ, the ship that also controlled the fighters), and the plane guard destroyer astern for the pilots who went into the water.
