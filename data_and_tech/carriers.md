# The carriers, and the three hulls

## Constellation (CV-64) vs Enterprise (CVN-65)

| | Enterprise | Constellation |
|---|---|---|
| Class | one of a kind | Kitty Hawk class |
| Power | 8 reactors (A2W) | 8 boilers, fuel oil |
| Fuel for the ship | none needed for years | about 7,800 tons of fuel oil; underway replenishment every 3–4 days at tempo |
| Fuel for the wing | more room, since none is needed for the ship | about 5,900 tons of aviation fuel |
| Air wing | up to about 100 | 80–90 |
| First combat | 2 December 1965, first nuclear warship in combat; 165 sorties in a day | 5 August 1964, Pierce Arrow |
| Accidents | 14 January 1969, off Hawaii: Zuni rocket cooked off, 28 dead, 314 injured, 15 aircraft | 19 December 1960, fire while building in Brooklyn, 50 workers dead; October 1969, helicopter lost over the side, 9 dead |
| At the fall of Saigon | present, Frequent Wind | in overhaul at Bremerton |
| Built | Newport News Shipbuilding, Virginia | New York Naval Shipyard, Brooklyn |

## The three hulls

| | Omaha (CL-4) | Missouri (BB-63) | Constellation (CV-64) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type | light cruiser, 1923 | fast battleship, 1944 | supercarrier, 1961 |
| Length | 555 ft | 887 ft | 1,047 ft |
| Crew | ~450 | ~2,700 | 5,000+ |
| Weapon | twelve 6-inch | nine 16-inch | 85 aircraft |
| Reach | ~14 mi | ~24 mi | hundreds of miles |
| Verdict | a scout | the peak of the gun, limited to it | a sovereign airbase |

Rating Constellation against the other two: the same steel, roughly, as Missouri (61,000 vs 45,000 tons), twenty-five times the reach, and a crew of a small town. The battleship's guns could reach 24 miles; her aircraft reached Hanoi.
