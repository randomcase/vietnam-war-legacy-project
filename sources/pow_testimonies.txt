THE TAP CODE

A 5x5 grid, the Polybius square, with K folded into C:

        1  2  3  4  5
    1   A  B  C  D  E
    2   F  G  H  I  J
    3   L  M  N  O  P
    4   Q  R  S  T  U
    5   V  W  X  Y  Z

Row taps, pause, column taps.  B = tap . tap-tap.  T = tap-tap-tap-tap . tap-tap-tap-tap.
GN = good night, through the walls every evening.  GBU = God bless you.
When the guards listened at the walls: sweep the floor, cough, thump a thigh, blink, on the same grid.
Introduced at Hoa Lo in 1965 by Smitty Harris, who had learned the square from an instructor at survival school.

STOCKDALE
  James B. Stockdale, shot down 9 September 1965, senior naval officer at Hoa Lo; seven and a half years.
  In Love and War (1984), with Sybil Stockdale: the code, the rope torture, the resistance rules (BACK US), and the
  wives' campaign at home that made the prisoners a public issue in 1969.

ALVAREZ
  Everett Alvarez Jr., A-4 from USS Constellation, shot down 5 August 1964 in Pierce Arrow; first Navy prisoner;
  eight years and six months; Chained Eagle (1989).

STIRM
  Robert L. Stirm, shot down 27 October 1967; released 14 March 1973; the Burst of Joy photograph at Travis, 17 March 1973;
  had received his wife's letter ending the marriage three days before the photograph.

McCAIN
  John S. McCain III, shot down 26 October 1967 over Hanoi; refused early release out of turn in 1968 when his father took
  Pacific Command; five and a half years.

HOMECOMING
  12 February to 4 April 1973; 591 returned in 54 C-141 flights; 2,646 then listed missing; 1,587 still unaccounted for in 2020.
