# Bibliography

Note: the brief credited *Burst of Joy* to "Sal Vder Slader". The photographer is **Sal Veder**, Associated Press; the photograph won the 1974 Pulitzer Prize for Feature Photography. The Agent Orange Act passed the House on 25 January 1991 and was signed into law on 6 February 1991 (Pub. L. 102-4); cite the enactment.
